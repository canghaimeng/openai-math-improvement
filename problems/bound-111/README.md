# An explicit one-sample matroid prophet guarantee

Developed with [Euler](https://github.com/canghaimeng/Euler), a mathematical research system for collaborative reasoning and human-guided exploration.

**[Read the paper](paper.pdf)**

The explicit guarantee is

$$C_{141}=\frac{165971304277649481670619}{324956758716416046811841770291200}>2^{-293}.$$

It applies to every finite matroid in the full-seed adversary model: the arrival order may depend on all samples, values and internal seed bits.

Using OpenAI's construction and Ryan Shea's quantitative refinements, the proof sharpens a conservative rational certificate through the estimate `B>141`. The paper gives the fixed-vector argument, lossless one-sample transfer and exact scalar inequalities.
