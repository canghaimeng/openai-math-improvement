# A sixth-degree bound for switch-chain mixing

Developed with [Euler](https://github.com/canghaimeng/Euler), a mathematical research system for collaborative reasoning and human-guided exploration.

**[Read the paper](paper.pdf)**

For the lazy four-vertex switch chain on graphs with any graphical degree sequence and `n ≥ 4`, the total-variation mixing time to accuracy `1/4` is at most **n⁶**, improving the original **2n⁸** bound under the same proposal convention.

The proof refines the comparison between local pair resampling and graph switches, then converts the spectral-gap estimate into a mixing-time bound. The paper includes the supporting pair-resampling argument and credits its source.
