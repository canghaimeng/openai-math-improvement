# A larger sensitivity–block sensitivity separation

Developed with [Euler](https://github.com/canghaimeng/Euler), a mathematical research system for collaborative reasoning and human-guided exploration.

**[Read the paper](paper.pdf)**

We construct an explicit infinite family of total Boolean functions with unbounded sensitivity and

$$\operatorname{bs}(f)\ge s(f)^\alpha,\qquad \alpha>\frac{380}{187}>2.032083.$$

The exponent improves OpenAI's displayed seed and its published depth-20 parameter estimate. The construction uses the nested-predicate architecture and ProveIt Part I's level-dependent schedule, retains the exact sensitivity envelope, and chooses 99 OR copies. Exact integer inequalities certify the exponent, and composition gives the infinite family.
